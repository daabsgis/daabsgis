About

Profile

I am Gideon Daabe, a Geoinformation Science professional specializing in Geographic Information Systems (GIS), Remote Sensing, spatial analysis, and location intelligence. I apply advanced earth observation datasets and technical modeling to deliver data-driven solutions for sustainable urban development, environmental monitoring, and climate-resilient asset management.

I am passionate about transforming complex data into practical solutions through interactive maps, field operations support, and decision workflows. My goal is to leverage geographic insights and remote sensing approaches to evaluate locations, recognize hidden patterns, and develop efficient tools for resource management and planning challenges.
