About Me

Profile

I am Gideon Daabe, a Geoinformation Science professional specializing in Geographic Information Systems (GIS), Remote Sensing, spatial analysis, and location intelligence. I apply advanced earth observation datasets, technical spatial modeling, and spatial analytics to deliver data-driven solutions for sustainable urban development, environmental monitoring, and climate-resilient asset management tasks.

I am highly passionate about transforming complex spatial datasets into practical solutions through interactive maps, field operations support, and decision workflows. My goal is to leverage geographic insights, remote sensing approaches, and spatial analytics to evaluate locations, recognize hidden patterns, and develop efficient tools for resource management and planning challenges.
